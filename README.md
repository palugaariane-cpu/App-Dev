# My Favorite Series: The Scum Villain's Self-Saving System

## About the Series
*The Scum Villain's Self-Saving System* is a Chinese danmei novel written by Mo Xiang Tong Xiu (MXTX). It combines fantasy, comedy, cultivation, and adventure.

## My Favorite Character
**Shen Qingqiu** is one of my favorite characters because he is intelligent, strategic, and complicated. His personality makes the story interesting.

## Why I Like This Series
I enjoy this series because:
- It has an interesting cultivation world.
- The characters have unique personalities.
- The story combines humor and drama.
- The plot has unexpected twists.

## My Favorite Things
1. The character development
2. The world-building
3. The relationships between characters

## Final Thoughts
Overall, this is an entertaining series with memorable characters and an interesting story. I would recommend it to readers who enjoy fantasy and cultivation stories.
